# Diagnose and repair

Diagnose problems and review repair plans, including verify and rebuild steps, in Management → Recovery. Destructive steps ask you to type the reviewed target's name.



## Steps

1. In Explorer Management, open **Recovery** and review the repair plan and its items.
2. To run a plan, type the first 12 characters of the plan id when asked; nothing runs until the typed text matches.
3. Follow progress in **Activity**.
