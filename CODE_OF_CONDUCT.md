# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Be considerate of differences in experience and background. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention and publishing private personal information are not welcome in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply these standards to issues, pull requests and other spaces operated by the project. Raise a conduct concern privately with a repository maintainer through an available private route; do not publish personal details or assume a particular contact channel exists.
