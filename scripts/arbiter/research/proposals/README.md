# Historical research proposals

Versioned source-preservation collections retain authored proposals without applying their behavior. [Native/client r1](native-client-r1/README.md) explains exact-byte mapping, historical limitations and verification. Every collection must document its own inputs, outputs and revival requirements; no folder is an execution or qualification permit.

[Standalone patch history r1](patch-history-r1/README.md) preserves authored patch inputs and separately accounts for authenticated upstream dependency patches. No patch is applied by preservation or verification.
